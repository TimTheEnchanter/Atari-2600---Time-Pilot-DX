# Time Pilot DX (Atari 2600)

An expanded, more arcade-like edition of Coleco's 1983 Time Pilot for the Atari 2600.
Cartridge: 16K F6 with Superchip RAM (F6SC). In Stella, use the `F6SC` cart type if it isn't auto-detected.

## Playing
- Press RESET to start. The joystick turns your plane through 16 directions, and the sky scrolls past it.
- Hold fire for autofire. Up to three of your shots can be on screen at once.
- Each era needs 56 kills. The progress bar and the plane icons at the bottom show how many are left.
- After 56 kills the era's boss appears, with a warning siren. Shoot it down to warp to the next era.
  Boss hit points by era: 6, 8, 8, 10 and 10. Damage you do to a boss stays if you lose a life.
- The eras are 1910, 1940, 1970, 1983 and 2001. After 2001 the game goes back to 1910.

## Arcade features added
- More enemies on screen without flicker, and V formations of three planes.
- Parachutists drift through the sky. Fly into one to rescue him: 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000, then 5000 points for
  each further rescue. The count starts over when you lose a life.
- From 1970 on, enemy shots are homing missiles. They turn toward you and burn out after about two seconds, and
  you can shoot them down for 100 points.
- Bosses drop aimed bombs. From 1970 on the bombs home too.
- Extra lives at 10,000 and 60,000 points.
- New title screen, a fanfare at the start of each life and era, jingles for extra lives and rescues, a boss siren,
  and a GAME OVER screen that takes turns with your final score.

## Scoring
Plane 100, missile 100, boss 3000, parachutist 1000-5000.

## Credits
Based on Time Pilot for the Atari 2600 (Coleco, 1983), licensed from Konami's 1982 arcade game.
